6. Legal Grounds for Processing

  • Legitimate interests: Processing necessary for legitimate operational, security or fraud-prevention interests, where permitted and balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: Processing based on your freely given consent where consent is required or relied upon.
  • Vital or public interests: Where a specific applicable law recognises such a basis.

The appropriate legal basis depends on the specific activity and the laws that apply to the responsible operator.
