Security & responsible account access

Use a strong, unique password, keep your sign-in details private and access CoinWave through the official website address. Never share passwords or one-time verification codes with anyone. Review transaction details carefully before submitting an order. Digital asset prices can change rapidly, and transactions may carry financial risk.

How transactions work

Transaction options are available after signing in. Depending on the service and asset, you may be asked to select an asset, enter an amount, review the displayed quote, fees and instructions, and confirm your request. A submitted request is not necessarily completed immediately; check its status in your account. Always follow the instructions shown on the transaction screen.

CoinWave customer support

For account-specific assistance, sign in and use the support options available in your customer account. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or private wallet keys to anyone claiming to provide support.
